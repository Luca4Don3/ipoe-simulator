# Code of Conduct

Project participants must communicate respectfully, focus criticism on technical work, respect privacy, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or publication of another person's data.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when behavior is unsafe, abusive, irrelevant, or exposes sensitive information. Serious platform abuse should also be reported through GitHub's abuse-reporting mechanisms.

Security vulnerabilities and privacy exposure must follow `SECURITY.md`; they must not be disclosed through public conduct reports or Issues.
